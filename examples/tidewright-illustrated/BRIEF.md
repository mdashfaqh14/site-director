# Creative brief: Tidewright

**What it is:** a fictional twelve-week writing residency in a working lighthouse. Six rooms, one lamp, no signal.
**Audience:** novelists and poets who need to finish something. They read slowly and distrust slick marketing.
**Goal:** make people feel the place in five seconds, then apply.

**Art direction:** Organic Warm, night edition (secondary: Retro-Futurist Terminal for the glyph moon and mono labels).
**Feel:** quiet, literary, a little playful. Motion personality: fluid.
**Palette:** hero night #070E16; everything below the hero in night sea #0E2230 / #0A1A25 / #123040 with cream #EFE4C8 text and lamp amber #F2A03D.
**Type:** Instrument Serif (display, with italics doing the emotional work), Instrument Sans (body), VT323 (labels, the keeper's log voice).

**Hero archetype:** illustrated world. A full-bleed scene drawn in code (asset strategy C): lighthouse, sea, moon reflection.
**Signature moment:** a playable night. The moon is written in lines of text (the residency's own lines), shaded by the moon's craters. Under the cursor the letters let go, pour down in a narrow amber stream, pile on the moon's lower edge and float back into their lines, while the moon hums like a singing bowl; the fireflies around the lamp ring like glass bells when touched. Both sounds share one scale (D major pentatonic), one 66 BPM grid and one echo, so they always play in time. Sound is off until the visitor turns it on.
**Supporting motion:** drifting embers and loose letters (particles), a sweeping lamp beam, twinkling stars, glinting reflection, a "Hide UI" pill to look at the picture.
**Second moment:** below the hero, the residency's mascot (a small octopus holding a quill) follows the cursor through every section, leaving a glowing moonlight ink trail.

**Layout rhythm:** illustrated hero → statement + three columns (paper) → seasons, three painted cards → journal quote (night) → apply with creature (paper) → footer.

**Recipes:** image-particles, particles (touchable), soundscape, cursor-creature (page mode), ui-toggle, text reveal (lines), clip reveal, stagger, magnetic, smooth scroll.

**Inspiration, not copying:** the "illustrated world + one cursor-reactive object" pattern and the "brand character as the cursor" pattern seen in Marina Budarina's Framer heroes (Nov 2026). Different subject, palette, type, layout, art and code.
